# 🚀 AI Career & Learning Platform

An all-in-one platform designed to help students and job seekers **prepare for interviews, improve their resumes, build professional portfolios, and access study materials** from a single place.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎤 Virtual Interview

Practice realistic virtual interviews with AI-powered interview sessions, questions, and feedback to improve your communication and interview performance.

### 📄 Resume Analyzer

Upload your resume and get an analysis of its structure, skills, keywords, and overall job-readiness, helping you identify areas for improvement.

### 💼 Portfolio Maker

Create a professional and responsive portfolio without building everything from scratch. Showcase your **skills, projects, education, experience, and achievements** in one place.

### 📚 Study Material

Access organized study materials and resources to support your learning and placement preparation.

## 🎯 Goal

The goal of this project is to provide students with a **complete career-preparation platform** where they can:

* Practice interviews
* Analyze and improve their resume
* Create a professional portfolio
* Learn from study materials
* Prepare for placements more effectively

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* Frontend: React.js
* Backend: Node.js & Express.js
* Database: MongoDB
* AI Integration: AI-powered analysis and interview features
* Authentication: JWT
* Styling: Tailwind CSS

## 🌟 Vision

To build a single platform that helps students go from **learning → preparing → building → applying → interviewing** without needing multiple separate tools.
